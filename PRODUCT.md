# Product

## Register

product

## Users

The developer/owner working at a desk beside their Mac, glancing at a 4.2-inch 400×300 1-bit monochrome reflective LCD from arm's length (50–80 cm) under ambient room light.

## Product Purpose

An always-glanceable desktop hardware instrument that shows weekly AI subscription Allowance (fuel road), daily, 7-day, and lifetime Token usage (30-day trend bars and odometer), and offline desk climate, while communicating stale, offline, link-failure, and sync-failure states truthfully.

## Brand Personality

Precision desk instrument (Braun industrial hardware meets Swiss railway clock): calm, disciplined, generous breathing room, and clear numeric hierarchy, preserving subtle playful micro-craft (the fuel-road car, the 30-day chart, Token Skyline, Desk Cat) without clutter or visual noise.

## Anti-references

- Cramped, colliding elements paired with unbalanced vertical voids around the edges.
- Drifting visual grammar across Pages (mismatched header/footer weights, inconsistent divider rules, arbitrary badge padding, or misaligned baselines).
- Heavy decorative card boxes or shrinking primary numbers in ways that sacrifice one-second arm's-length legibility.

## Design Principles

1. **One-second arm's-length glanceability**: Primary numbers and status must read immediately from 50–80 cm without visual hunting.
2. **One instrument grammar across Pages**: Allowance and Token share identical chrome proportions, divider weights, chip padding, and alignment datums.
3. **Honest vertical rhythm**: Expand breathing room when fewer rows are active while staying collision-free at maximum row capacity.
4. **Truthful telemetry over polish**: Offline, stale, link-failed, and sync-failed states stay unmistakable without hiding retained values.
5. **Restraint in 1-bit ink**: Create structure through alignment, whitespace, typographic weight, and dot-density hierarchy instead of heavy borders.

## Accessibility & Inclusion

- Target medium is a 4.2-inch 400×300 1-bit monochrome reflective LCD (~120 DPI) with zero grayscale or color encoding and high ambient-light dependency.
- Light polarity (black ink on white paper) is the default, with optional live polarity inversion; neither mode may rely on thin sub-pixel contrast or crowded 1-px collisions.
- Zero element overlap under worst-case strings, inverted low-fuel callouts, and maximum provider counts (4 rows on Allowance, Token, and Station).
- Animation is strictly bounded to USB-connected `HOST` mode (entry sweep, gentle fuel-road motion, Token Skyline sky motion, and Desk Cat); `ACTIVE` and `ECO` render crisp static frames.
